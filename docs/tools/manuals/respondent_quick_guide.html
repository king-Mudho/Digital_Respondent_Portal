ABF-FST research study · Chinhoyi University of Technology

How to complete and submit the questionnaire

  • About 15–25 minutes
  • Phone, tablet or computer
  • No app to install, no account to create
  • Voluntary: you may stop at any time

Before you start

  • Your personal linkSent to you by WhatsApp, SMS or email. It begins research.agribizframework.com/i/
  • For your organisation onlyIt works for 14 days. Please don’t forward it; ask us for a new one if it has expired.
  • A steady connectionAnd 15–25 minutes without interruption. We will never ask for a password, bank details or payment.

Step by step

  1. Open your link

    Tap the link in your message. The study page opens in your browser.

  2. Confirm your organisation

    If the name shown is yours, tap Yes, that’s correct.Not your organisation? Stop here and contact us.

  3. Tell us a little about you

    Type Your full name, choose the answer to Which best describes your role?, then tap Continue.If no role fits, choose None of these describe me and tell us who should answer.

  4. Read the participant information

    It explains the study, how your information is protected and your rights. Then tap Continue to consent.

  5. Give your consent

    Tap I agree to take part.Prefer not to? Tap I do not wish to take part. That is completely fine.

  6. Check what is already public (only if shown)

    On Before we continue, mark each item Yes, correct, Partly correct, No — correct it, Don’t know, Prefer not to say or Not applicable, then Continue. Or tap Skip this step.

  7. Choose how to take part

    Tap Complete it myself now, then Start the questionnaire.Would like help? Choose Have a researcher call me or Complete it via WhatsApp instead.

  8. Answer and submit

    The questionnaire opens on KoboToolbox, the study’s secure form service. Answer each question and tap Next. On the last page tap Submit, not Save Draft. The form jumps back to its first page, then a banner at the top says it was successfully submitted. You are done.

Make sure your answers arrive

  • Always start from your personal link, never from a saved or forwarded questionnaire page. Only answers started from your link can be matched to your organisation.
  • Submit won’t work? Look for a question marked in red: it still needs an answer.
  • Your answers are sent only when you tap Submit. The thank-you text on the last page appears before that, and Save Draft keeps your answers on your device only.
  • Submit once. The empty form that appears after submitting is normal. Wait for the “successfully submitted” banner, don’t fill the form in again, then close the page.
  • Questionnaire won’t open? Tap Try again, or Ask a researcher to help me instead. Interrupted? Open your link again; if you cannot carry on, contact us and a researcher will help.

Your answers are for research only. No score, rating or financing decision is produced, and your individual answers are never shared with any lender. Results are reported only in combined, anonymised form.
